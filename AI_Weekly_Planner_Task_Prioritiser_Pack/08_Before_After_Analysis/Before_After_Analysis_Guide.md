# Before-and-after analysis guide

Use one real baseline week where you planned normally and one real AI-assisted week. Keep measures and task-count definitions consistent. If you do not have baseline records, collect them prospectively or clearly label the comparison as unavailable; do not fabricate a baseline.

For completion rate:
`(completed tasks ÷ tasks planned) × 100`

For change in completion rate:
`AI-assisted completion rate − baseline completion rate`
Report this in **percentage points**, not as percent growth.

Discuss whether the weeks were comparable: task difficulty, deadlines, class load, illness, interruptions and total available time. A single-week result is a small personal experiment, not proof that AI always improves productivity.
